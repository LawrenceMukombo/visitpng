# Visit PNG — Connecting People to PNG 🇵🇬

**Visit PNG — Your Digital Guide to Papua New Guinea**

Visit PNG is a locally developed mobile tourism platform designed to make exploring Papua New Guinea easier, more convenient, and more accessible.

We provide visitors, tourists, and locals with a digital travel pocket guide to discover accommodation, restaurants, attractions, tours, activities, events, and other tourism services across PNG — all in one place.

At the same time, Visit PNG helps local businesses, hotels, restaurants, tour operators, small businesses, and tourism operators promote their services, reach new customers, and increase their online visibility.

---

## What We Deliver

- 📍 **Discover**: Find places to stay, eat, visit, and explore across all 22 provinces.
- 🏨 **Accommodation**: Connect visitors with hotels, lodges, and other stays.
- 🍽️ **Dining**: Discover restaurants, cafés, and local food experiences.
- 🌿 **Experiences**: Find attractions, tours, activities, and places of interest (Kokoda Track, Coral Triangle diving, Mount Wilhelm, Sepik River).
- 📣 **Business Promotion**: Give PNG businesses a digital platform to showcase their products and services.
- 🌏 **Tourism Connectivity**: Connect visitors with local tourism operators and experiences.
- 🇵🇬 **Promoting PNG**: Help showcase Papua New Guinea as a destination to both local and international visitors.

> **Our Goal**: Make it easier for people to discover PNG, while helping PNG businesses become more visible, accessible, and connected in the digital tourism space.

---

## Platform Architecture
- **Stewardship**: Developed & maintained by **LanFrame** (Port Moresby, Papua New Guinea)
- **Framework**: Next.js (App Router), React, TypeScript
- **Database**: SQLite / Cloudflare D1 with Drizzle ORM
- **Mobile**: Android Trusted Web Activity (TWA) with Google Play Store compliance
- **Typography & Design**: Ubuntu typography tokens and rich responsive palettes
