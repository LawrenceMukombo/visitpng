1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to VisitPNG ("we," "our," or "us"), operated by VisitPNG Tourism Services Ltd, registered in Port Moresby, Papua New Guinea. VisitPNG is committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of our users ("you" or "traveler").

This Privacy Policy applies to the VisitPNG mobile application on Google Play (Package: com.visitpng.travel) and our web platform at https://visitpng.lamtoninvestments.com. It describes how we collect, store, process, protect, and handle your information in full compliance with the Papua New Guinea Data Protection & Digital Government Framework, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Google Play Developer Policies.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data strictly necessary to deliver verified tourism guides and safe navigation across PNG's 22 provinces:

  • Location Data (Foreground Only): With your runtime permission, we use approximate or precise GPS to show nearby lodges, attractions, Kokoda Track waypoints, and center the GIS map. We do not track location in the background when the app is closed.
  • Account Information: Name, email address, and phone number when voluntarily registering.
  • Travel Wishlists & Itineraries: Saved itineraries and offline emergency guides stored locally on your device for low-connectivity expeditions.

3. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You can delete your account and personal data anytime in-app under Profile > Settings > Delete Account or by emailing privacy@visitpng.com. Deletion requests are fulfilled within 48 hours.
